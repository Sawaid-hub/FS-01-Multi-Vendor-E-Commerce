# Stock photo credits

Market Lane uses remote product and maker photography from Unsplash for the local frontend preview. Images are loaded from Unsplash CDN URLs in `client/src/App.jsx` and are not bundled with the repository. Each image links to its original Unsplash photo page below. The photography is used under the Unsplash License; review that license and source page if imagery is redistributed outside this demo.
